Step 2

Guess the album

A random album from your library — guess its name from a pixelated cover, its release year, 5 of its songs, then put the whole tracklist in order.

Tracklists come from last.fm first (your key above), with Apple's free iTunes lookup filling in cover art and release year, since last.fm's own data for those two is inconsistent. Very obscure or mistagged albums may occasionally fail to match either source — if one does, the game just tries another from your pool.
